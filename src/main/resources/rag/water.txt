BILLBUDDY WATER BILLING KNOWLEDGE BASE

==================================================
1. WATER USAGE
==================================================

Water consumption can be measured using units such as kilolitres (kL).

For BillBuddy, water usage may be represented in kilolitres.

Example:

Usage = 25 kL

This represents the recorded water consumption during the billing
period.


==================================================
2. WATER BILL AMOUNT
==================================================

A water bill may contain several components depending on the provider
and applicable billing rules.

Possible components include:

- Water consumption charges.
- Fixed charges.
- Service charges.
- Sewerage or related charges.
- Taxes.
- Fees.
- Billing adjustments.

The exact composition depends on the water provider.

BillBuddy should rely on the actual bill data when available instead
of assuming that every bill contains all of these components.


==================================================
3. WATER USAGE INCREASE
==================================================

An increase in water usage means that the recorded water consumption
is higher during the current billing period than during a previous
period.

Possible contributing factors include:

- Increased household water consumption.
- Changes in daily usage.
- More frequent use of water-consuming activities.
- Seasonal changes.
- Changes in occupancy.
- Irrigation or outdoor water use.
- Changes in consumption behavior.

These are possible explanations only.

BillBuddy must not state that a specific factor caused the increase
without supporting evidence.


==================================================
4. WATER BILL AMOUNT INCREASE
==================================================

A higher water bill may result from:

- Increased water consumption.
- Changes in applicable rates.
- Fixed charges.
- Service charges.
- Taxes or fees.
- Billing adjustments.
- Changes in the billing period.

A higher bill amount does not automatically mean that water consumption
increased.

BillBuddy should compare both the bill amount and water usage.


==================================================
5. COST PER UNIT
==================================================

For BillBuddy analysis, calculated water cost per unit can be expressed
as:

Cost per unit = Total bill amount / Water usage

When water usage is measured in kilolitres:

Cost per unit = Total bill amount / Usage in kL

Example:

Total bill = 850
Usage = 25 kL

Cost per unit = 850 / 25
              = 34 per kL

This calculated value is an analytical measure.

It may not represent the official water tariff because the total bill
may contain fixed charges, taxes, fees, or other components.


==================================================
6. WATER BILL ANOMALIES
==================================================

BillBuddy may detect the following anomalies in water bills:

AMOUNT_SPIKE

A significant increase in the total water bill amount compared with
the previous water bill.

BillBuddy threshold:
20 percent or greater increase.


USAGE_SPIKE

A significant increase in recorded water consumption compared with
the previous water bill.

BillBuddy threshold:
20 percent or greater increase.


COST_PER_UNIT_SPIKE

A significant increase in calculated water cost per kilolitre compared
with the previous water bill.

BillBuddy threshold:
15 percent or greater increase.


ZERO_USAGE_NONZERO_AMOUNT

The water bill contains zero recorded usage while the total bill
amount is greater than zero.

Possible explanations include:

- Fixed charges.
- Minimum charges.
- Service charges.
- Taxes or fees.
- Billing adjustments.
- Missing or incorrect usage information.

BillBuddy must not assume that the bill is incorrect without additional
evidence.


==================================================
7. INTERPRETING WATER BILL CHANGES
==================================================

BillBuddy should compare:

1. Previous water bill amount.
2. Current water bill amount.
3. Previous water usage.
4. Current water usage.
5. Previous cost per kL.
6. Current cost per kL.

Example:

Previous:
Amount = 850
Usage = 25 kL
Cost per unit = 34

Current:
Amount = 1100
Usage = 32 kL
Cost per unit = 34.375

Interpretation:

The water bill amount increased.

Water usage also increased from 25 kL to 32 kL.

The calculated cost per unit increased only slightly.

The available bill data therefore shows both increased consumption and
an increase in the total bill amount.

The data alone does not establish the exact reason for the increased
water consumption.


==================================================
8. WHEN WATER USAGE DOES NOT INCREASE
==================================================

If water consumption remains approximately the same while the bill
amount increases significantly, possible explanations may include:

- Changes in applicable charges.
- Fixed charges.
- Service charges.
- Taxes or fees.
- Billing adjustments.
- Rate changes.

These should be presented as possibilities rather than confirmed
causes unless supporting information is available.


==================================================
9. WATER BILL VERIFICATION
==================================================

When a water bill appears unusual, the user may verify:

- Recorded water usage.
- Billing period.
- Previous usage reading.
- Current usage reading.
- Applicable rates.
- Fixed charges.
- Service charges.
- Taxes and fees.
- Billing adjustments.

The user may also compare the current bill with previous bills to
identify whether the change is temporary or part of a longer-term
pattern.

BillBuddy should present these as verification suggestions.


==================================================
10. WATER USAGE AND POSSIBLE LEAKS
==================================================

An unusual increase in water usage may sometimes be associated with
higher consumption or a possible leak.

However, increased usage alone does not prove that a leak exists.

BillBuddy should not state that a leak occurred unless supporting
evidence is available.

A safer explanation is:

"The recorded water usage increased significantly. A leak is one
possible explanation, but the available bill data cannot confirm this."


==================================================
11. EVIDENCE RULE
==================================================

BillBuddy explanations should distinguish between:

FACT:
Information directly available from the water bill.

INFERENCE:
A calculation or comparison derived from the available data.

POSSIBLE CAUSE:
An explanation that requires additional evidence.

Example:

FACT:
Water usage increased from 25 kL to 32 kL.

INFERENCE:
Water usage increased by 28 percent.

POSSIBLE CAUSE:
Higher household consumption or another factor could have contributed.

The possible cause must not be presented as a confirmed explanation
without supporting evidence.
